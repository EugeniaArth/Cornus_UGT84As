# Literature and source records for the UGT84 dataset

This catalog identifies the literature, accessions, and local records used to assemble the protein dataset. The project includes 32 curated protein sequences: 24 UGT84 A/B/J references, six UGT74 Group-L comparators, and two family-1 context proteins. Per-sequence names, lengths, source headers, and membership/function evidence are recorded in `sequences/formal_ugt84_reference_manifest.tsv`, `sequences/groupL_ugt74_comparator_seeds.faa`, and `sequences/family1_context_outgroups.faa`.

## Group classification and comparator records

Hoffmann et al. (2023, Table 2) maps UGT71/72/88 to Group E, UGT74/75/84 to Group L, and UGT76 to Group H. This broad classification supports the use of UGT74 sequences as Group-L comparators to UGT84; it does not establish a specific enzyme function or a direct sister relationship for each sequence.

The six Group-L comparator accessions and exact record labels are AEC10333.1 (UGT74F1), AEC10331.1 (UGT74F2), ALO19895.1 (CsUGT74B4), ALO19896.1 (CsUGT74B5), ALO19897.1 (CsUGT74AF1), and ALO19898.1 (CsUGT74AG1). Their saved protein records are under `sources/ncbi_records/protein/` and their curated sequences are in `sequences/groupL_ugt74_comparator_seeds.faa`.

The context sequences are AAW56092.1 (Medicago UGT71G1; Group E) and AAR06912.1 (Stevia UGT76G1; Group H), saved in `sequences/family1_context_outgroups.faa` and as individual accession FASTAs under `sources/ncbi_records/protein/`. UGT76G1 is not a Group-E sequence. No UGT72 or UGT88 protein is included.

Their protein accessions and source FASTAs are listed in the sequence manifest and `sources/ncbi_records/protein/`. Complete PubMed XML/citation metadata for all of these additional PMIDs is not present in this source collection; verify the references before manuscript submission.

The file `derived/ugt84_members_from_2024_review_supplement_pages3-4.txt` contains page 3 only despite its filename. It should not be cited as a complete extraction of pages 3–4.

## Candidate-screen profile and provenance

Pfam PF00201 is available through the InterPro entry <https://www.ebi.ac.uk/interpro/entry/pfam/PF00201/>. It is a broad UDP-glycosyltransferase-family profile for candidate recovery, not a UGT84 classifier. The profile file is not included because the exact build and redistribution conditions have not been verified. Any future HMMER run should record the local profile hash, command, software version, and raw outputs.

## Access and reuse

PubMed XML is the preferred saved citation metadata. Use PMC full-text XML and original supplements rather than HTML snapshots when checking article content. The license and reuse terms of every article and supplement have not been independently recorded. Local possession does not establish permission to redistribute full text, figures, supplements, or sequence records; verify the applicable terms before external release. Experimental evidence applies only to the specific proteins and assay conditions reported in each cited study.
